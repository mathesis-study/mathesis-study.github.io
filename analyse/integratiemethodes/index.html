Integratiemethodes

Leerplandoelstellingen (GO! Navigator)

Gevorderde wiskunde (WD3_06.08)

  • • WD3_06.08.32 (MD 06.08.21) De leerlingen berekenen bepaalde en onbepaalde integralen van functies.

    • – afbakening: integratiemethoden: onmiddellijke integratie, integratie door splitsing (lineariteit), integratie door substitutie, partiële integratie

Didactische uitwerking: onbepaalde integraal als verzameling van primitieve functies, basisintegralen, lineariteit van de integraal, integratie door splitsing, eenvoudige substitutie, partiële integratie, en toepassingen van deze integratiemethodes op algebraı̈sche, exponentiële, goniometrische en logaritmische functies.

I Onbepaalde integraal

1 Definitie

Is \(F\) een primitieve functie van \(f\), dan wordt de verzameling van alle primitieve functies van \(f\),

\[ \{\,F + C \mid C \in \mathbb {R}\,\}, \]

de onbepaalde integraal van \(f\) genoemd.

\[ \int f(x)\,dx \;=\; \{\,F + C \mid C \in \mathbb {R}\,\}. \]

Hierbij heet \(f(x)\) het integrandum en \(\int \) het integraalteken.

2 Eigenschappen

  • • \(\displaystyle d\!\left (\int f(x)\,dx\right ) = d(F(x)) = F'(x)\,dx = f(x)\,dx. \)
    Dus: \(d\) na \(\int \) heffen elkaar op.

  • • \(\displaystyle \int dF(x) = \int F'(x)\,dx = \int f(x)\,dx = F(x) + C. \)
    Dus: \(\int \) na \(d\) heffen elkaar op (op een constante term na).

\(\Rightarrow \) Integreren en differentiëren zijn inverse bewerkingen!

3 Rekenregels
\(\seteqnumber{0}{}{0}\)

\begin{align*} \int (f+g)\,dx \;&=\; \int f\,dx \;+\; \int g\,dx\\ \int k\,f(x)\,dx \;&=\; k\int f(x)\,dx \end{align*}

Meer algemeen, voor \(a,b\in \mathbb {R}\):

\[ \int \bigl (a\,f(x)+b\,g(x)\bigr )\,dx \;=\; a\int f\,dx \;+\; b\int g\,dx. \]

Maar:

\(\seteqnumber{0}{}{0}\)

\begin{align*} \int f\cdot g\,dx \;&\neq \; \left (\int f\,dx\right )\left (\int g\,dx\right )\\ \int \frac {f}{g}\,dx \;&\neq \; \frac {\int f\,dx}{\int g\,dx}. \end{align*} zie later bij partiële integratie.

II Basisintegralen

Zie ook HB Analyse 4 p. 71. Fundamentele integralen

Parate kennis!!

\(\seteqnumber{0}{}{0}\)

\begin{align*} \int 1\,dx \;&=\; x + C\\[2mm] \int x^{n}\,dx \;&=\; \frac {x^{n+1}}{n+1} + C \qquad (n\in \mathbb {R}\setminus \{-1\})\\[2mm] \int \frac {1}{x}\,dx \;&=\; \ln |x| + C\\[2mm] \int a^{x}\,dx \;&=\; \frac {a^{x}}{\ln a} + C \qquad (a>0,\ a\neq 1)\\[2mm] \int e^{x}\,dx \;&=\; e^{x} + C\\[2mm] \int \sin x\,dx \;&=\; -\cos x + C\\[2mm] \int \cos x\,dx \;&=\; \sin x + C\\[2mm] \int \frac {1}{\cos ^{2}x}\,dx \;&=\; \tan x + C\\[2mm] \int \frac {1}{\sin ^{2}x}\,dx \;&=\; -\cot x + C\\[2mm] \int \frac {1}{\sqrt {1-x^{2}}}\,dx \;&=\; \arcsin x + C \;=\; -\arccos x + C\\[2mm] \int \frac {1}{1+x^{2}}\,dx \;&=\; \arctan x + C \;=\; -\operatorname {arccot} x + C\\[2mm] \int \frac {1}{\sqrt {x^{2}+k^{2}}}\,dx \;&=\; \ln \!\left |x+\sqrt {x^{2}+k^{2}}\right | + C \qquad (k\neq 0)\\[2mm] \int \sinh x\,dx \;&=\; \cosh x + C\\[2mm] \int \cosh x\,dx \;&=\; \sinh x + C \end{align*}

III Substitutiemethode

1 Geval 1: \(t=ax+b\)

Neem

\[ t=ax+b \quad \Rightarrow \quad dt=a\,dx \quad \Rightarrow \quad dx=\frac 1a\,dt. \]

Voorbeeld 1
\(\seteqnumber{0}{}{0}\)

\begin{align*} \int \sin (x+3)\,dx &= \int \sin t\,dt \qquad (t=x+3,\ dt=dx)\\ &= -\cos t + C\\ &= -\cos (x+3)+C. \end{align*}

Praktisch:

\[ \int \sin (x+3)\,dx=\int \sin (x+3)\,d(x+3)=-\cos (x+3)+C. \]

Voorbeeld 2
\(\seteqnumber{0}{}{0}\)

\begin{align*} \int e^{-2x+1}\,dx &= \int e^{t}\left (-\frac 12\,dt\right ) \qquad (t=-2x+1,\ dt=-2\,dx)\\ &= -\frac 12\int e^{t}\,dt\\ &= -\frac 12 e^{t}+C\\ &= -\frac 12 e^{-2x+1}+C. \end{align*}

Praktisch:

\[ \int e^{-2x+1}\,dx = -\frac 12\int e^{-2x+1}\,d(-2x+1) = -\frac 12 e^{-2x+1}+C. \]

Voorbeeld 3
\(\seteqnumber{0}{}{0}\)

\begin{align*} \int \sin ^2 x\,dx &= \int \frac {1-\cos 2x}{2}\,dx\\ &= \frac 12\int 1\,dx - \frac 12\int \cos 2x\,dx\\ &= \frac 12 x - \frac 12\cdot \frac 12\sin 2x + C\\ &= \frac 12 x - \frac 14\sin 2x + C\\ &= \frac 12 x - \frac 14(2\sin x\cos x) + C\\ &= \frac {x-\sin x\cos x}{2}+C. \end{align*}

\[ \int \cos ^2 x\,dx=\frac {x+\sin x\cos x}{2}+C \qquad \text {(analoog).} \]

Voorbeeld 4
\(\seteqnumber{0}{}{0}\)

\begin{align*} \int \frac {1}{a^{2}+x^{2}}\,dx &= \frac {1}{a^{2}}\int \frac {1}{1+\left (\frac {x}{a}\right )^{2}}\,dx\\ &= \frac {1}{a^{2}}\cdot a \int \frac {1}{1+\left (\frac {x}{a}\right )^{2}}\,d\!\left (\frac {x}{a}\right )\\ &= \frac {1}{a}\,\arctan \!\left (\frac {x}{a}\right )+C. \end{align*}

\(\seteqnumber{0}{}{0}\)

\begin{align*} \int \frac {1}{\sqrt {a^{2}-x^{2}}}\,dx &= \frac {1}{a}\int \frac {1}{\sqrt {1-\left (\frac {x}{a}\right )^{2}}}\,dx\\ &= \frac {1}{a}\cdot a \int \frac {1}{\sqrt {1-\left (\frac {x}{a}\right )^{2}}}\,d\!\left (\frac {x}{a}\right )\\ &= \arcsin \!\left (\frac {x}{a}\right )+C \qquad (a>0). \end{align*}

\[ \int \frac {1}{a^{2}+x^{2}}\,dx=\frac {1}{a}\,\arctan \!\left (\frac {x}{a}\right )+C \]

\[ \int \frac {1}{\sqrt {a^{2}-x^{2}}}\,dx=\arcsin \!\left (\frac {x}{a}\right )+C \qquad (a>0). \]

2 Geval 2: \(t=\text {noemer}\)

Indien de integranda een breuk is, waarvan de teller de afgeleide is van de noemer:

\[ \int \frac {f'(x)}{f(x)}\,dx \qquad \text {met}\qquad t=f(x)\ \Rightarrow \ dt=f'(x)\,dx, \]

dan geldt

\[ \int \frac {f'(x)}{f(x)}\,dx =\int \frac {dt}{t} =\ln |t|+C =\ln |f(x)|+C. \]

Voorbeeld 1
\(\seteqnumber{0}{}{0}\)

\begin{align*} \int \frac {e^{x}}{e^{x}+5}\,dx &= \int \frac {dt}{t} \qquad (t=e^{x}+5,\ dt=e^{x}\,dx)\\ &= \ln |t|+C\\ &= \ln |e^{x}+5|+C. \end{align*}

Voorbeeld 2
\(\seteqnumber{0}{}{0}\)

\begin{align*} \frac {1}{3}\int \frac {3x^{2}}{x^{3}+5}\,dx &= \frac {1}{3}\int \frac {dt}{t} \qquad (t=x^{3}+5,\ dt=3x^{2}\,dx)\\ &= \frac {1}{3}\ln |t|+C\\ &= \frac {1}{3}\ln |x^{3}+5|+C. \end{align*}

3 Geval 3: \(\displaystyle \int f(x)^n\,f'(x)\,dx \ \Rightarrow \ t=f(x)\)

Als de integraal de vorm heeft

\[ \int f(x)^n\,f'(x)\,dx, \qquad \text {neem dan } t=f(x)\ \Rightarrow \ dt=f'(x)\,dx. \]

Dan wordt

\[ \int f(x)^n\,f'(x)\,dx=\int t^n\,dt=\frac {t^{n+1}}{n+1}+C =\frac {f(x)^{n+1}}{n+1}+C \qquad (n\neq -1). \]

Voorbeeld 1
\(\seteqnumber{0}{}{0}\)

\begin{align*} \int \frac {\cos x}{\sin ^{5}x}\,dx &= \int \sin ^{-5}x\cdot \cos x\,dx\\ &= \int t^{-5}\,dt \qquad (t=\sin x,\ dt=\cos x\,dx)\\ &= \frac {t^{-4}}{-4}+C\\ &= -\frac {1}{4t^{4}}+C\\ &= -\frac {1}{4\sin ^{4}x}+C. \end{align*}

Voorbeeld 2
\(\seteqnumber{0}{}{0}\)

\begin{align*} \int (x^{3}-2x+1)^{4}\,(3x^{2}-2)\,dx &= \int t^{4}\,dt \qquad (t=x^{3}-2x+1,\ dt=(3x^{2}-2)\,dx)\\ &= \frac {1}{5}t^{5}+C\\ &= \frac {1}{5}(x^{3}-2x+1)^{5}+C. \end{align*}

IV Partiële integratie

1 Opstellen regel

Beschouw 2 afleidbare functies:

\[ f:\mathbb {R}\to \mathbb {R}: x\mapsto f(x), \qquad g:\mathbb {R}\to \mathbb {R}: x\mapsto g(x). \]

Dan geldt (productregel):

\[ d(f\cdot g)=df\cdot g + f\cdot dg. \]

Hieruit volgt:

\(\seteqnumber{0}{}{0}\)

\begin{align*} &&f\,dg &= d(fg) - g\,df\\ \Leftrightarrow && \int f\,dg &= \int d(fg) - \int g\,df\\ \Leftrightarrow && \int f\,dg &= fg - \int g\,df. \end{align*}

\[ \boxed {\int f\,dg = f\,g - \int g\,df} \]

Opmerkingen

  • • Bijna integratie van een ’product’ !

  • • De bedoeling is een integraal te vinden die makkelijker te berekenen is dan de gegeven integraal.

2 Type \(\int x^n\ln x\,dx\) en \(\int x^n\arctan x\,dx\)

1) \(\displaystyle \int \ln x\,dx\)

Kies bij partiële integratie:

\[ f=\ln x \qquad \text {en}\qquad dg=dx. \]

Dan:

\[ df=\frac {1}{x}\,dx \qquad \text {en}\qquad g=\int dx=x. \]

Dus:

\(\seteqnumber{0}{}{0}\)

\begin{align*} \int \ln x\,dx &= \int f\,dg\\ &= f\,g - \int g\,df\\ &= x\ln x - \int x\cdot \frac {1}{x}\,dx\\ &= x\ln x - \int 1\,dx\\ &= x\ln x - x + C. \end{align*}

Opmerking: \(C\) wordt toegevoegd als er geen andere constanten zijn.

2) \(\displaystyle \int x^{2}\ln x\,dx\)

Kies:

\[ f=\ln x \qquad \text {en}\qquad dg=x^{2}\,dx. \]

Dan:

\[ df=\frac {1}{x}\,dx \qquad \text {en}\qquad g=\int x^{2}\,dx=\frac {1}{3}x^{3}. \]

Dus:

\(\seteqnumber{0}{}{0}\)

\begin{align*} \int x^{2}\ln x\,dx &= f\,g-\int g\,df\\ &= \frac {1}{3}x^{3}\ln x-\int \frac {1}{3}x^{3}\cdot \frac {1}{x}\,dx\\ &= \frac {1}{3}x^{3}\ln x-\frac {1}{3}\int x^{2}\,dx\\ &= \frac {1}{3}x^{3}\ln x-\frac {1}{3}\cdot \frac {1}{3}x^{3}+C\\ &= \frac {1}{9}x^{3}\,(3\ln x-1)+C. \end{align*}

3) \(\displaystyle \int \arctan (3x)\,dx\)

Kies bij partiële integratie:

\[ f=\arctan (3x) \qquad \text {en}\qquad dg=dx. \]

Dan:

\[ df=\frac {3}{1+9x^{2}}\,dx \qquad \text {en}\qquad g=\int dx=x. \]

Dus:

\(\seteqnumber{0}{}{0}\)

\begin{align*} \int \arctan (3x)\,dx &= f\,g-\int g\,df\\ &= x\,\arctan (3x)-\int x\cdot \frac {3}{1+9x^{2}}\,dx\\ &= x\,\arctan (3x)-3\int \frac {x}{1+9x^{2}}\,dx. \end{align*}

Substitutie:

\[ t=1+9x^{2}\quad \Rightarrow \quad dt=18x\,dx \quad \Rightarrow \quad x\,dx=\frac {1}{18}\,dt. \]

Dan:

\(\seteqnumber{0}{}{0}\)

\begin{align*} 3\int \frac {x}{1+9x^{2}}\,dx &= 3\int \frac {1}{t}\cdot \frac {1}{18}\,dt\\ &= \frac {1}{6}\int \frac {1}{t}\,dt\\ &= \frac {1}{6}\ln |t|+C\\ &= \frac {1}{6}\ln (1+9x^{2})+C. \end{align*}

Conclusie:

\[ \int \arctan (3x)\,dx = x\,\arctan (3x)-\frac {1}{6}\ln (1+9x^{2})+C. \]

Oefeningen

p110 nr1

3 Type \(\int x^n e^x\,dx\)

1) \(\displaystyle \int x\,e^x\,dx\)

Kies:

\[ f=x \qquad \text {en}\qquad dg=e^x\,dx. \]

Dan:

\[ df=dx \qquad \text {en}\qquad g=\int e^x\,dx=e^x. \]

Dus:

\(\seteqnumber{0}{}{0}\)

\begin{align*} \int x\,e^x\,dx &= f\,g-\int g\,df\\ &= x\,e^x-\int e^x\,dx\\ &= x\,e^x-e^x+C\\ &= e^x(x-1)+C. \end{align*}

4 Type \(\int x^n\cos x\,dx\) en \(\int x^n\sin x\,dx\)

1) \(\displaystyle \int x\cos x\,dx\)

Kies:

\[ f=x \qquad \text {en}\qquad dg=\cos x\,dx. \]

Dan:

\[ df=dx \qquad \text {en}\qquad g=\int \cos x\,dx=\sin x. \]

Dus:

\(\seteqnumber{0}{}{0}\)

\begin{align*} \int x\cos x\,dx &= f\,g-\int g\,df\\ &= x\sin x-\int \sin x\,dx\\ &= x\sin x+\cos x+C. \end{align*}

2) \(\displaystyle \int x^{2}\sin x\,dx\)

Eerst partiële integratie:

\[ f=x^{2} \qquad \text {en}\qquad dg=\sin x\,dx. \]

Dan:

\[ df=2x\,dx \qquad \text {en}\qquad g=\int \sin x\,dx=-\cos x. \]

\(\seteqnumber{0}{}{0}\)

\begin{align*} I=\int x^{2}\sin x\,dx &= f\,g-\int g\,df\\ &= -x^{2}\cos x-\int (-\cos x)\,2x\,dx\\ &= -x^{2}\cos x+\int 2x\cos x\,dx. \end{align*}

Nogmaals partiële integratie op \(J=\int 2x\cos x\,dx\):

\[ f=2x,\quad dg=\cos x\,dx \qquad \Rightarrow \qquad df=2\,dx,\quad g=\sin x. \]

\(\seteqnumber{0}{}{0}\)

\begin{align*} J &= 2x\sin x-\int \sin x\cdot 2\,dx\\ &= 2x\sin x-2\int \sin x\,dx\\ &= 2x\sin x+2\cos x + C. \end{align*}

Dus:

\[ \int x^{2}\sin x\,dx = -x^{2}\cos x+2x\sin x+2\cos x + C. \]

5 Type \(\displaystyle \int \sqrt {x^{2}+k}\,dx\) en \(\displaystyle \int \sqrt {k-x^{2}}\,dx\)

Voorbeeld 1 Bereken

\[ I=\int \sqrt {x^{2}+5}\,dx. \]

Oplossing Partiële integratie met

\[ f=\sqrt {x^{2}+5},\quad dg=dx \qquad \Rightarrow \qquad df=\frac {x}{\sqrt {x^{2}+5}}\,dx,\quad g=x. \]

Dan:

\(\seteqnumber{0}{}{0}\)

\begin{align*} I &= x\sqrt {x^{2}+5}-\int x\,d\!\left (\sqrt {x^{2}+5}\right )\\ &= x\sqrt {x^{2}+5}-\int x\cdot \frac {x}{\sqrt {x^{2}+5}}\,dx\\ &= x\sqrt {x^{2}+5}-\int \frac {x^{2}}{\sqrt {x^{2}+5}}\,dx\\ &= x\sqrt {x^{2}+5}-\int \frac {x^{2}+5-5}{\sqrt {x^{2}+5}}\,dx\\ &= x\sqrt {x^{2}+5}-\int \frac {x^{2}+5}{\sqrt {x^{2}+5}}\,dx +5\int \frac {1}{\sqrt {x^{2}+5}}\,dx\\ &= x\sqrt {x^{2}+5}-\underbrace {\int \sqrt {x^{2}+5}\,dx}_{=\,I} +5\int \frac {1}{\sqrt {x^{2}+5}}\,dx. \end{align*}

In het rechterlid verschijnt opnieuw de te zoeken integraal.

Voor de laatste integraal gebruiken we de basisintegraal:

\[ \int \frac {1}{\sqrt {x^{2}+k^{2}}}\,dx = \ln \!\left |x+\sqrt {x^{2}+k^{2}}\right |+C. \]

Hier is \(k^{2}=5\), dus:

\[ \int \frac {1}{\sqrt {x^{2}+5}}\,dx = \ln \!\left |x+\sqrt {x^{2}+5}\right |+C. \]

Bijgevolg:

\[ 2I=x\sqrt {x^{2}+5}+5\ln \!\left |x+\sqrt {x^{2}+5}\right |+C \]

en dus:

\[ \boxed { I=\frac 12\,x\sqrt {x^{2}+5}+\frac 52\,\ln \!\left |x+\sqrt {x^{2}+5}\right |+C }. \]

6 Type \(\int e^x\sin x\,dx\) en \(\int e^x\cos x\,dx\)

1) \(\displaystyle \int e^x\cos x\,dx\)

Zet

\[ I=\int e^x\cos x\,dx. \]

Eerste partiële integratie:

\[ f=e^x,\quad dg=\cos x\,dx \qquad \Rightarrow \qquad df=e^x\,dx,\quad g=\sin x. \]

\(\seteqnumber{0}{}{0}\)

\begin{align*} I &= e^x\sin x-\int e^x\sin x\,dx. \end{align*}

Noem

\[ J=\int e^x\sin x\,dx. \]

Tweede partiële integratie:

\[ f=e^x,\quad dg=\sin x\,dx \qquad \Rightarrow \qquad df=e^x\,dx,\quad g=-\cos x. \]

\(\seteqnumber{0}{}{0}\)

\begin{align*} J &= -e^x\cos x+\int e^x\cos x\,dx\\ &= -e^x\cos x+\underbrace {\int e^x\cos x\,dx}_{=\,I}. \end{align*}

Invullen in de eerste vergelijking:

\(\seteqnumber{0}{}{0}\)

\begin{align*} I &= e^x\sin x - J\\ &= e^x\sin x -\Bigl (-e^x\cos x+\underbrace {\int e^x\cos x\,dx}_{=\,I}\Bigr )\\ &= e^x\sin x + e^x\cos x - I. \end{align*}

Dus:

\[ 2I=e^x(\sin x+\cos x) \qquad \Rightarrow \qquad I=\frac 12 e^x(\sin x+\cos x)+C. \]

Methode 2: goniometrische als \(f\) en exponentiële met \(dx\) als \(dg\) (zelfde opgave)

Neem opnieuw

\[ I=\int e^x\cos x\,dx. \]

Eerste partiële integratie:

\[ f=\cos x,\quad dg=e^x\,dx \qquad \Rightarrow \qquad df=-\sin x\,dx,\quad g=e^x. \]

\(\seteqnumber{0}{}{0}\)

\begin{align*} I &= f\,g-\int g\,df\\ &= e^x\cos x-\int e^x(-\sin x)\,dx\\ &= e^x\cos x+\int e^x\sin x\,dx. \end{align*}

Noem

\[ J=\int e^x\sin x\,dx. \]

Tweede partiële integratie:

\[ f=\sin x,\quad dg=e^x\,dx \qquad \Rightarrow \qquad df=\cos x\,dx,\quad g=e^x. \]

\(\seteqnumber{0}{}{0}\)

\begin{align*} J &= e^x\sin x-\int e^x\cos x\,dx\\ &= e^x\sin x-\underbrace {\int e^x\cos x\,dx}_{=\,I}. \end{align*}

Invullen:

\(\seteqnumber{0}{}{0}\)

\begin{align*} I &= e^x\cos x+J\\ &= e^x\cos x+\Bigl (e^x\sin x-\underbrace {\int e^x\cos x\,dx}_{=\,I}\Bigr )\\ &= e^x(\cos x+\sin x)-I. \end{align*}

Dus:

\[ 2I=e^x(\sin x+\cos x) \qquad \Rightarrow \qquad I=\frac 12 e^x(\sin x+\cos x)+C. \]

7 Recursieformules voor \(\int \sin ^n x\,dx\) en \(\int \cos ^n x\,dx\)

1) Recursie voor \(\displaystyle I_n=\int \sin ^n x\,dx\)

Zet

\[ I_n=\int \sin ^n x\,dx=\int \sin ^{n-1}x\;\sin x\,dx. \]

Kies bij partiële integratie:

\[ f=\sin ^{n-1}x,\quad dg=\sin x\,dx \qquad \Rightarrow \qquad df=(n-1)\sin ^{n-2}x\cos x\,dx,\quad g=-\cos x. \]

Dan:

\(\seteqnumber{0}{}{0}\)

\begin{align*} I_n &= f\,g-\int g\,df\\ &= -\sin ^{n-1}x\cos x+\int \cos x\,(n-1)\sin ^{n-2}x\cos x\,dx\\ &= -\sin ^{n-1}x\cos x+(n-1)\int \sin ^{n-2}x\cos ^2x\,dx\\ &= -\sin ^{n-1}x\cos x+(n-1)\int \sin ^{n-2}x(1-\sin ^2x)\,dx\\ &= -\sin ^{n-1}x\cos x+(n-1)\underbrace {\int \sin ^{n-2}x\,dx}_{=\,I_{n-2}} -(n-1)\underbrace {\int \sin ^{n}x\,dx}_{=\,I_n}. \end{align*}

Dus:

\[ nI_n=-\sin ^{n-1}x\cos x+(n-1)I_{n-2} \]

en de recursieformule wordt:

\[ \boxed { I_n=-\frac {1}{n}\sin ^{n-1}x\cos x+\frac {n-1}{n}\,I_{n-2} } \qquad (n\ge 2). \]

2) Recursie voor \(\displaystyle J_n=\int \cos ^n x\,dx\)

Zet

\[ J_n=\int \cos ^n x\,dx=\int \cos ^{\,n-1}x\;\cos x\,dx. \]

Kies:

\[ f=\cos ^{\,n-1}x,\quad dg=\cos x\,dx \qquad \Rightarrow \qquad df=-(n-1)\cos ^{\,n-2}x\sin x\,dx,\quad g=\sin x. \]

Dan:

\(\seteqnumber{0}{}{0}\)

\begin{align*} J_n &= f\,g-\int g\,df\\ &= \cos ^{\,n-1}x\sin x+(n-1)\int \cos ^{\,n-2}x\sin ^2x\,dx\\ &= \cos ^{\,n-1}x\sin x+(n-1)\int \cos ^{\,n-2}x(1-\cos ^2x)\,dx\\ &= \cos ^{\,n-1}x\sin x+(n-1)\underbrace {\int \cos ^{\,n-2}x\,dx}_{=\,J_{n-2}} -(n-1)\underbrace {\int \cos ^{\,n}x\,dx}_{=\,J_n}. \end{align*}

Dus:

\[ nJ_n=\cos ^{\,n-1}x\sin x+(n-1)J_{n-2} \]

en:

\[ \boxed { J_n=\frac {1}{n}\cos ^{\,n-1}x\sin x+\frac {n-1}{n}\,J_{n-2} } \qquad (n\ge 2). \]

Voorbeeld: \(\displaystyle I_3=\int \sin ^3 x\,dx\)

\(\seteqnumber{0}{}{0}\)

\begin{align*} I_3 &= -\frac {1}{3}\sin ^{2}x\cos x+\frac {2}{3}I_{1}\\ &= -\frac {1}{3}\sin ^{2}x\cos x+\frac {2}{3}\int \sin x\,dx\\ &= -\frac {1}{3}\sin ^{2}x\cos x-\frac {2}{3}\cos x + C\\ &= -\frac {1}{3}\bigl (\sin ^{2}x\cos x+2\cos x\bigr )+C. \end{align*}
